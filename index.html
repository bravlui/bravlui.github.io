About me

Where disciplines
meet and challenge each other

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at São Paulo State University (UNESP) and hold a Bachelor's degree in Statistics and Data Science from the University of São Paulo (USP).

Rather than viewing Artificial Intelligence as a single discipline, I see it as a meeting point between statistics, optimization, networks, information retrieval, and human reasoning.

Can uncertainty improve representation learning?

How can context be incorporated into similarity learning?

Can probabilistic reasoning improve ranking and retrieval?

How can graphs enrich visual and semantic representations?

Featured projects

Information Retrieval · Fairness

Query Fairness Estimation Under Demographic Data Scarcity

Experimental pipeline evaluating Query Fairness Estimation methods (Naive@k, CC, PACC, KDEy) under low availability of demographic labels, extending Jaenich et al. (2026) to correction pools of 100–10,000 labeled documents over 162,200 documents and 25 queries from trec-fair/2022. Proposes dRAE, a rank-discounted extension of RAE, and shows that recalibrating the sensitive-attribute classifier via temperature scaling cuts KDEy's error from 0.792 to 0.094.

Network Science

Graph Sampling — Preserving Network Structure Under Subsampling

Systematic study of six network sampling methods across three classic synthetic graph models (Erdős–Rényi, Barabási–Albert, Watts–Strogatz) and two network sizes, evaluating how well each method preserves the original topology using KL, JS, Wasserstein, and KS distance metrics. Coursework for Complex Networks and Dynamic Processes (ICMC-USP).
